	Game:  simulates games of pokemon between two planners using an engine.
		run:  Play games of pokemon between two planners.
		rollout:  Compute terminal event probabilities given a starting state and planners.
	Planner:  dictate player behavior
		methods:
			evaluateGamma:  evaluates a chance node. States are recursed in order of their probability.
			evaluateAlphaBeta:  evaluates the choices of both agents. Other choice attempts to minimize fitness, Agent choice is the maximum of other choices. States are recursed in order of their conditional average fitness increase.
			cutoffs:
				child.upperBound <= highCutoff.lowerBound - cannot do better than worst other cutoff
				child.lowerBound >= lowCutoff.upperBound - cannot to worse than best agent cutoff
				lowCutoff.upperBound >= highCutoff.lowerBound - gamma window size is negative
		submodules:
			Evaluator:  - evaluates a game state's fitness
				extends:
					EvaluatorSimple:  - human-engineered evaluator
					EvaluatorPerceptron:  - uses a perceptron to compute fitness
					EvaluatorMonteCarlo:  - evaluates a state given successive rollouts.
		extends:
			PlannerNegamax:  evaluates moves in order of nested probability (best-first traversal)
			PlannerMinimax:  - uses expectiminimax to determine optimal move
				submodules:
					KillerHeuristic:  The first move attempted is the one that produced the strongest cutoff in the transposition table
					OrderHeuristic:  For a given pair of agent/other pokemon, visit actions in the order most likely to produce a cutoff.
					TranspositionTable:  recalls evaluated performance of a given state. May recall full and partial evaluations.
					ply:  an agent move, other move, all possible random outcomes of those moves:
			PlannerMaximin:  testing module. Simplest minimax algorithm capable of evaluating N-depth.
			PlannerMax:  testing module. Determines the locally optimal move, not taking into account the other agent's choice.
			PlannerStochastic:  performs a move in accordance to a probability distribution
			PlannerHuman:  prints status, accepts moves from STDIN (PY)
